# Mobile Bartending & Event Service Management Software: Customer Feedback Research

**Research Date:** November 21, 2025
**Focus:** Event management, catering, mobile bar, and bartending service software

---

## Executive Summary

This research analyzes customer feedback from multiple sources including G2, Capterra, Software Advice, App Store reviews, TrustPilot, and industry forums. The report covers major platforms including HoneyBook, Dubsado, Planning Pod, Caterease, Better Cater, CaterZen, Total Party Planner, and Cvent.

**Key Finding:** 67.4% of event planners have already switched or are planning to switch their event software due to platform failures, lack of features, or poor engagement capabilities.

## 2. What Users HATE About Existing Solutions

### Major Pain Points (1-2 Star Reviews)

#### **Steep Learning Curve & Complexity**
- **Dubsado:** "Has a severe learning curve" - "an active Facebook group exists because it's so hard to set up that people literally specialize in Dubsado setup as part of their career"
- **Dubsado:** "Very clunky - users struggle understanding what sections or categories to find things or how to properly set them up—it's very overwhelming even for tech-savvy users"
- **Cvent:** "High learning curve which provides difficulties setting up the event for first time organizers"
- **Planning Pod:** "Many settings and key components can be hard to find in the web interface"

#### **Poor Customer Support**
- **Dubsado:** "It can take a long time to get help with a problem. You can only send email and wait hours for a reply. They rarely ever answer chat."
- **Better Cater:** "At $500 per year, no one ever answers the phone when you have a question"
- **HoneyBook:** "Very buggy, it takes weeks for the HoneyBook team to resolve them, and the customer service is not supportive at all"
- **Planning Pod:** "Customer service representatives seemed unfamiliar with the platform and often gave incomplete answers"

#### **Outdated Technology & UI**
- **Caterease:** "Just so outdated" - "an antiquated system" - "one of the longer-standing names in the industry, and this is reflected in the archaic user experience"
- **Caterease:** "Connection server issues arising everyday" - "Users have to rely on the speed of their Citrix server to get anything done (which isn't the best)"
- **Planning Pod:** "UI is described as 'a bit clunky'" - "clients find the features too confusing/complicated to use"

#### **Limited Customization**
- **HoneyBook:** "The biggest downside to HoneyBook is its lack of customization"
- **Better Cater:** "Very little in terms of document customization—which happens to be a common complaint among users"
- **Cvent:** "Customization is essential for many events. Lack of customization options could make the software less versatile for unique needs"
- Catering software generally: "Template limitations where pre-set templates might not be customizable to fit unique event requirements"

#### **Technical Glitches & Reliability Issues**
- **HoneyBook:** "The platform is very buggy" - "feature issues with reporting, automations, and project date syncing can be problematic"
- **Planning Pod:** "Frequently getting red 'caution' symbols and unreliable saving when changes are made"
- **Caterease:** "The Citrix system being down or inoperable impeded days of business where users couldn't retrieve their BEOs"
- **BarMasters Mobile Bartending:** "Push notifications have never worked to alert them for new events, requiring them to open the app every few hours"

#### **Mobile App Limitations**
- **Better Cater:** "Unfortunately, there's no mobile app, which would streamline the process of adding and tracking events on the go"
- **Curate:** "It's annoying to create proposals on the limited mobile app"
- **Dubsado:** "Doesn't have an app (yet) & that doesn't seem to be something on the immediate roadmap"
- **Android disparity:** "The Android functionalities in the app have gotten better over the past year, but still lacking some of the features that Apple users have"

#### **Poor Project Management**
- **Dubsado:** "The project management aspect needs improvement, and the client portal isn't that great, forcing users to use different project management tools"
- **Dubsado:** "Is not a Project Management app, and even though Dubs has a lite task manager built-in, it's probably not going to offer the features you need"

## 3. Common 1-Star and 2-Star Review Complaints

### Financial Issues

#### **Pricing Too High**
- **HoneyBook:** "Honestly I like the service however almost $600 is wildly overpriced for what I am receiving"
- **HoneyBook:** "It was just too expensive for my start up" - "Price is pretty expensive for small businesses"
- **HoneyBook:** "Too confusing and too expensive"
- **Dubsado:** "Overall it feels a bit clunky and perhaps too expensive and heavy for businesses"
- **Cvent:** "Cost often considered steep for many organizations" - "Particularly for smaller companies"

#### **Transaction Fees & Hidden Costs**
- **HoneyBook:** "Users criticize the expensive costs and high transaction fees, feeling trapped and frustrated with limited data export options"
- **HoneyBook:** "Complaints about Smart File proposal receipts not showing a net profit to easily calculate the transaction fee"
- **Caterease:** "Not uncommon for Caterease users to feel nickel-and-dimed as they charge extra for many features customers would consider standard such as a document builder and lead tracking tool"

#### **Price Increases**
- **HoneyBook:** "Disappointed in the huge price increase especially when these AI heavy updates are not when I need or use"
- **HoneyBook:** "Some users downgraded their plan in preparation for the price increase coming later this month because paying for features I don't need or use is crazy"

### Payment Processing Disasters

#### **HoneyBook Payment Horror Stories**
- "If a person files a dispute, HoneyBook goes into your bank account and removes all money from any booked contract, without warning and holds it for up to 90 days, and they also charge a dispute fee"
- "HoneyBook is not depositing clients' money into their account and it's taking at least 2 weeks to get their money"

#### **Invoice Errors**
- **Dubsado:** "One user reported that Dubsado tried to charge 100 times more than what they issued an invoice for"

### Technical Failures

#### **Data Loss & Saving Issues**
- **Caterease:** "Users have to save everything along the way or risk losing work if they accidentally close windows"
- **Planning Pod:** "Unreliable saving when changes are made"
- **Better Cater:** "When printing an invoice, the name shows from the previous customer"

#### **Integration Problems**
- **Planning Pod:** "Calendar sync does not work properly, making it useless for scheduling appointments"
- **Planning Pod:** "Integration with Microsoft 365 relies on Zapier with a very limited set of tools available"
- **Planning Pod:** "Planning Pod does not have an API available"
- **Better Cater:** "Inability to import previous contacts and menus"

#### **Workflow Limitations**
- **Dubsado:** "Users wish Dubsado would incorporate if/then options into workflows and have folders inside workflows and templates"
- **Dubsado:** "The scheduler can't mass update availability, and once a scheduler is added to a project, you have to manually update availability"
- Event management generally: "Processing delays in some cases with slight delays in the execution of automated workflows"

### Reporting Deficiencies

- **Caterease:** "Reporting is 'Very limited, very complicated, and overall useless'"
- **Better Cater:** "Reporting offerings somewhat limited—yet easy to use"
- **HoneyBook:** "Companies seek HoneyBook alternatives due to factors such as limited reporting capabilities"

### Usability Issues

#### **Printing Problems**
- **Caterease:** "Because the application runs through Citrix, printing is very complicated (even worse with wireless printing)"
- **Caterease:** "Users wish emails through Caterease would hold attachments"

#### **Multi-Step Processes**
- **Caterease:** "Several reviewers have mentioned that Caterease is not intuitive and requires multiple steps to complete tasks"
- **Caterease:** "Even starting the program takes about 6 different steps"
- **Better Cater:** "Having to add line items one by one - no syncing means manual input of items, which is time consuming"

#### **Security Annoyances**
- **Caterease:** "The system has a forced password reset every two weeks and doesn't store passwords"

### Mobile App Specific Complaints

#### **BarMasters Mobile Bartending (iOS/Android)**
- "Push notifications never worked to alert them for new events, requiring them to manually check the app every few hours"
- "Events often don't show up or are already claimed"
- "Low pay and no notifications for events in your area"

#### **Tend - Premier Hospitality (iOS)**
- "Events are unorganized, and most events have floor captains who are unsure or late"
- "Difficult to be accepted to multiple shifts and even more difficult to get a bartender shift"
- "Disorganization, lack of jobs compared to other apps like Qwick and Instawork"

#### **Other App Issues**
- **Poached Jobs:** "App glitches including exit buttons not working and inability to open messages from jobs"
- Event apps generally: "Customers have complained about the lack of features and technical issues on the mobile app"

### Client-Facing Issues

#### **Proposal & Accept Button Problems**
- "The major worrying complaint that pops up time and time again is the 'Accept proposal' button not working for clients"
- General proposal software: "You can't sign it electronically, hard to get it looking right, you'll spend your life formatting it"

#### **Client Portal Limitations**
- **Dubsado:** "The client portal isn't that great"
- **Planning Pod:** "Clients find the features too confusing/complicated to use"

## Key Insights & Patterns

### Critical Success Factors
1. **Responsive customer support** (mentioned most frequently in 5-star reviews)
2. **Automation that actually works** (huge time saver)
3. **Mobile accessibility** (increasingly non-negotiable)
4. **True all-in-one solution** (reduces software juggling)
5. **Professional client-facing materials** (reflects on user's business)

### Deal Breakers
1. **Poor payment processing** (especially holding funds, delayed deposits)
2. **Price increases without value** (especially for features not used)
3. **Unreliable technology** (crashes, data loss, slow performance)
4. **Steep learning curve without payoff** (complexity without benefit)
5. **Feeling trapped** (difficult data export, locked into high fees)

### Emerging Trends (2024-2025)
1. **AI-powered automation** becoming standard expectation
2. **Mobile-first** vs mobile-friendly - apps are expected, not optional
3. **Transparent pricing** - users hate hidden fees and nickel-and-diming
4. **White glove migration** - reduces switching friction
5. **Real-time analytics** - data-driven decision making
6. **Self-service vs support dependency** - users want control

### Market Gaps Identified
1. **Mid-tier pricing** - gap between expensive enterprise (Cvent) and limited budget options
2. **Easy but powerful** - most are either too simple or too complex
3. **Mobile bartending specific** - most solutions are general event management
4. **True if/then automation** - basic workflows exist but conditional logic lacking
5. **Beautiful AND functional** - often it's one or the other
6. **Proposal creation that works** - "Accept button not working" is chronic issue

## Recommendations for New Product Development

Based on this research, a successful mobile bartending/event service platform should:

1. **Start mobile-first** - Full-featured native apps, not just responsive web
2. **Transparent, fair pricing** - No surprise fees, no holding customer funds
3. **Quick wins out of the box** - Professional templates that work immediately
4. **Progressive complexity** - Easy to start, powerful when needed
5. **Exceptional support** - Fast response times, knowledgeable team
6. **Smart automation** - If/then workflows, not just linear sequences
7. **Beautiful client experience** - Professional appearance reflects on user's brand
8. **Easy migration** - Import from competitors, easy data export
9. **Reliable technology** - Fast, stable, doesn't lose data
10. **Fair value exchange** - Features users actually need at prices that make sense

### Critical Features Priority Order
1. Mobile apps (iOS/Android) with full functionality
2. Payment processing that works reliably
3. Automated workflows with conditional logic
4. Professional templates (contracts, proposals, invoices)
5. Client portals that are actually easy to use
6. Real-time calendar sync (no double bookings)
7. Robust reporting and analytics
8. Easy customization (branding, templates)
9. Quick, helpful customer support
10. Fair, transparent pricing
