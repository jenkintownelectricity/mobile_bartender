## 7. KEY INSIGHTS & RECOMMENDATIONS

### Software Selection Patterns

**Small/Solo Mobile Bartenders**
- Prefer all-in-one solutions (Wheelo, Check Cherry)
- Use freemium tools (Wave, Sling Free, Square Free)
- Minimize number of platforms

**Growing Mobile Bartending Businesses**
- Combine HoneyBook + Dubsado for client management
- Add specialized scheduling (Sling, Connecteam)
- Invest in proper accounting (QuickBooks, FreshBooks)
- Use Square or Stripe for payments

**Established Companies**
- Enterprise-level solutions
- Multiple integrated platforms
- Custom pricing arrangements
- Comprehensive inventory management

### Common Pain Points

Based on research:
- Need for integrated solutions (many platforms required)
- Push notification reliability issues in some apps
- Event visibility and claiming issues
- Payment collection challenges at events
- Staff scheduling and no-show problems (solved by Roosted)
- Time-consuming inventory processes (solved by scanning apps)

### Competitive Advantages of Good Software

- 80-85% reduction in inventory time
- 50% to under 5% reduction in no-shows (with automation)
- Faster payment collection
- Professional client experience
- Automated marketing and follow-up
- Real-time profitability tracking
- Mobile-first operations
